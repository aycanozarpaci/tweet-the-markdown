# Privacy Policy — Tweet to Markdown

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Tweet to Markdown does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What the extension stores.** Your formatting preferences and the Markdown you copied
are saved with the browser's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) on your own
computer. They are used only to show your copy history and to apply your settings.

**What leaves your device.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests, contains
no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no remote code. There are no user
accounts.

**Deleting your data.** Use "Clear all" in the extension's History tab, or remove the
extension — both erase everything it stored.

**Permissions.** "storage" keeps your settings and history locally. "clipboardWrite" puts
the generated Markdown on your clipboard. The extension runs only on x.com and
twitter.com pages that you open yourself.
